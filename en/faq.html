Frequently asked questions

Answers to what our customers ask most. Didn't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

Does the software work offline?

Yes. The software and its data are on your computer, so it works normally without internet. A connection is only needed to renew the subscription and for updates.

Where is my data kept?

On your own computer; we never see it. The software makes automatic backups on the schedule you choose, and you can export them anywhere.

How does the free trial work?

Use the full software free for 7 days. Then send us your computer ID (shown in the software) and we send you an activation code once you subscribe.

Is the subscription monthly or yearly?

Yearly. Starting 30 days before it ends, the software reminds you daily to renew.

What happens to my data when the subscription ends?

Nothing is ever deleted. The software pauses until you renew, then everything is back as it was.

Can I move the software to another computer?

Yes. Contact us and we activate it on the new computer; bring your data over with a backup.

How do I pay?

By Sham Cash, Syriatel Cash, MTN Cash, bank transfer or cash. Message us on WhatsApp to subscribe.

Can you make a template for my business?

Yes. Tell us how your business works and we'll prepare a template for it in BrimoSoft Invoicing.

What's the difference between BrimoSoft Invoicing and BrimoSoft Al-Jadeer?

Invoicing handles invoices and payments, with templates for your business (workshop, fabric plant…). The Accountant is a full accounting and warehouse system: cashier, stock and counts, VAT, several currencies, entries and financial reports.

Does the Accountant support VAT?

Yes. Set the rate and whether prices include it; each product can have its own rate, and the VAT return covers any period.

What is “Sign in” at the top of the site?

The BrimoSoft portal for subscribers: the business owner follows the subscription, computers, managers and their permissions, and downloads the software. Your invoices and accounts themselves stay on your computer.

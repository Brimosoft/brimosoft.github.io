FAQ

Quick answers

Does the software work offline?

Yes. The software and its data are on your computer, so it works normally without internet. A connection is only needed to renew the subscription and for updates.

Where is my data kept?

On your own computer; we never see it. The software makes automatic backups on the schedule you choose, and you can export them anywhere.

How does the free trial work?

Use the full software free for 7 days. Then send us your computer ID (shown in the software) and we send you an activation code once you subscribe.

Is the subscription monthly or yearly?

Yearly. Starting 30 days before it ends, the software reminds you daily to renew.

What happens to my data when the subscription ends?

Nothing is ever deleted. The software pauses until you renew, then everything is back as it was.

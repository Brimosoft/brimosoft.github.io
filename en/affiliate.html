Questions about the affiliate programme

What's the difference between a marketing partner and a referral marketer?

A marketing partner contacts companies and follows up their customers personally, and gets 30% of every subscription and renewal. A referral marketer (influencers, social media marketers) shares a code and gets 20% one time per customer.

How long does a marketing partner's commission continue?

As long as they bring at least one new customer every month, and their customers stay subscribed.

What happens if I stop bringing new customers?

After 3 months without a new customer your account is suspended and the ongoing commissions stop. It can be reactivated later by contacting us and signing a new marketer agreement, but commissions on earlier customers don't come back; they start again on new customers only.

What is a referral marketer's commission based on?

20%, one time, of the first year's subscription, or of the price of a permanent licence if the customer buys the software outright.

When is the commission paid?

30 days after the customer pays.

Who gets the commission if a customer came through more than one person?

The owner of the first code recorded for that customer.

What isn't allowed?

No commission on your own subscription. You may not give customers a discount out of your own pocket, promise features our software doesn't have, send spam in BrimoSoft's name, or pose as BrimoSoft staff. Breaking these suspends the account and its commissions.

Commissions follow the affiliate programme's terms, which we send you in writing when you join.

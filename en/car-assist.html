AI Scanner: car fault diagnosis app

A workshop assistant on your phone or on the scan tool's tablet: it analyses faults with AI, reads codes, stays beside you over the Autel app, and carries the Diesel Programmer video library and its faults table.

Ask for the app

An AI assistant with voice and photos

Ask by typing or speaking, send a photo of the scan screen, or go live so it sees the camera and hears you.

Diagnoses and PDF reports

A card for each customer, car, complaint and photos, saved offline, then analysed into a report ready to share.

Over 12,000 fault codes

A code library that works offline, and a Mitsubishi L200 / Triton guide with yes/no test steps.

A floating window over the scan tool

«Analyse the screen» reads the Autel app's screen and explains it, plus a quick codes page, without leaving the scan app.

The Diesel Programmer library

156 videos about the Autel MS906 with Arabic and English titles, the lessons and the transcript of each.

102 faults and their fixes

Symptoms, code, diagnosis, fix and the video, searchable by symptom or code.

Runs on Android, on phones and on the scan tool's tablet. AI analysis and the videos need internet.

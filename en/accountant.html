What it offers

Accounting and warehouses in one program

Everything a trading business needs in one program, in clear sections.

Sales and cashier

  • Cash, credit and part-paid invoices, and returns
  • A fast barcode cashier with thermal receipts
  • Sale orders and installment sales
  • Price levels: retail, wholesale, half-wholesale
  • Sales reps and their commissions
  • A credit limit per customer, and customer groups

Purchases and stock

  • Purchase invoices with shipping spread over the cost
  • Several warehouses and transfers between them
  • Weighted average cost and a movement card per product
  • Stock counts, adjustments and damage notes
  • A reorder list that becomes a purchase order in one click
  • Product pictures, brands and barcode labels

Finance and accounting

  • Chart of accounts, with an entry for every document (Pro)
  • Several cash boxes, receipts, payments and expenses
  • VAT and the VAT return
  • Several currencies, exchange rates and differences
  • Fixed assets and monthly depreciation (Pro)
  • Owner withdrawals, capital and year-end close (Pro)

Reports

  • Income statement, balance sheet and trial balance
  • Customer and supplier debt ageing
  • Product and customer profitability
  • Expenses by type and stock valuation
  • Print any report or export it to Excel

Control and safety

  • Users with detailed permissions
  • Approvals: what's beyond an employee waits for a manager (Pro)
  • Activity log, and a recycle bin that keeps 30 days
  • Automatic backups
  • Import products and customers from Excel
  • A4 and thermal print templates with your logo

Car rental

  • Fleet management: each car with its month's calendar, free, rented, booked or in maintenance
  • The Syrian rental offices' contract on one A4 page: renter, guarantor, driver, terms and signatures
  • Car returns with lateness, km and fuel worked out; the deposit given back or kept
  • Bookings with a deposit and its receipt, and a warning when a booking's day comes unconfirmed
  • Pictures of the car and its gauges on delivery and return; PDF and WhatsApp sharing
  • Fines, damages, maintenance and a revenue report per car

Clinics and HR

  • Each doctor's appointments for the day, visits and patient files
  • Consultation fees, services and supplies from stock
  • The doctor's commission goes to their payroll
  • Employees, attendance, advances, leave and payroll
